# Contributing to Kowloon

Thanks for wanting to help. Kowloon is small and opinionated, so please read
this before you start.

## Four ground rules

**1. Don't be a jerk.** No bigotry, racism, harassment, or cruelty, toward
anyone, anywhere the project lives. The [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
has the details.

**2. Talk before big changes.** Open an issue or a Discussion before you start
anything bigger than a bug fix or a small, obvious improvement. Expect to explain
why your change is needed and to defend it. Unless it's plainly good, it won't
be merged just because the code works.

**3. People come before profit, every time.** Kowloon puts the people using it
ahead of any commercial interest, with no exceptions. That means no ads, no
tracking, no engagement tricks, no selling or mining people's data, and no
features that serve a business at its users' expense. A change that trades
users' interests for money will be turned down, however well it's built.

**4. The maintainer decides.** Josh Ellis has the final say on what goes in.
Disagreement is welcome and gets a real hearing, but when a call has to be
made, he makes it.

## Practical bits

- Bug reports: say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead,
  and which server and app version you were using.
- Security problems: don't open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Keep pull requests small and about one thing.
- Kowloon is licensed under Apache-2.0. By contributing, you agree your work is
  released under the same licence.
